Calendar integration

Simple, reliable calendar synchronization.

WMT Schedule Sync is a limited-access utility that automates the creation, maintenance and sharing of calendars and calendar events.

Calendar management

Create dedicated calendars and maintain event information as underlying records change.

Automated updates

Synchronize event titles, dates and times, and remove obsolete events when needed.

Controlled sharing

Manage access to individual calendars for designated recipients.

How it works

The utility connects to Google Calendar through the Google Calendar API using authorization from its designated account administrator. Calendar access is used to create and update events, identify existing events for synchronization, and manage calendar sharing permissions.

This integration is intended for limited, authorized use. It does not offer public registration or require calendar recipients to grant the utility access to their personal Google accounts.

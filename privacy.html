Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains how WMT Schedule Sync (the utility) handles information when providing its calendar synchronization and sharing functions.

1. Information accessed

With the designated Google account administrator's authorization, the utility accesses Google Calendar data needed for calendar management. Depending on the operation, this includes:

  • Calendar identifiers, names and configuration information.
  • Event identifiers, titles, dates, times and other event details used for synchronization.
  • Calendar access-control entries, recipient email addresses and sharing permissions.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens required to authenticate API requests.

The utility is not intended to access recipients' personal calendars, email messages or unrelated Google account information.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and maintain calendars, reconcile existing events with updated source records, manage access permissions, and diagnose synchronization errors. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold to data brokers, or used to create advertising profiles.

3. Storage and protection

Calendar content is stored in Google Calendar. The utility also maintains operational records, including calendar and event identifiers, recipient information and synchronization history, to support reliable updates. Authorization credentials and operational records are kept in the utility's operating environment and are intended to be accessible only to authorized administrators. API communication with Google uses HTTPS.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Calendar information is shared through Google Calendar with designated recipients according to administrator-configured permissions. The utility uses Google as the calendar service provider; authorized administrators may access information as necessary to operate, troubleshoot or support the utility. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Retention, deletion and revocation

Operational information is retained as needed for calendar synchronization, administration and applicable recordkeeping obligations. To request removal of your information or calendar access, contact the administrator at the address below. Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable obligations and technical limitations. The authorizing account holder may revoke OAuth access through Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further authorized API operations but does not automatically delete existing calendars or stored records.

6. Website and external services

This public information website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information as part of operating its service. These pages do not request Google authorization or contain a public registration form.

7. Google API data policy

WMT Schedule Sync's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the utility or its data practices. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact contact@wiseatc.com.
